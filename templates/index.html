01 ABOUT
BUSINESS OPERATIONS ANALYST

Not only an analyst.
An operator, problem-solver and builder.

My current work sits close to the operating engine: pricing, routes, FX, margins, API/service performance, reconciliation, accounting controls, reporting, agent support and management analysis.

Alongside recurring operations, I design and implement internal tools that make those processes more structured and repeatable — from local BI and rate workflows to reconciliation controls and decision-support applications.

I also support partner/service investigations, prepare evidence and error-ratio analysis, follow up on technical and pricing issues, and turn recurring operational questions into dashboards, reports, workflows or automation.

“Understand the business first.
Then build the system.”
02 OPERATIONS
WHAT I DO

Business operations is the
foundation of the work.

The portfolio now reflects the recurring operational responsibilities behind the applications — not just the applications themselves.

01PRICING & COMMERCIAL CONTROL

Pricing · Routes · FX · Margin

Maintain pricing sheets and pricing rules across Airtime, Data, Bundles and Pins; review market rates, API margins, route conditions and margin issues; support route decisions using FX, errors and commercial conditions.

02API & SERVICE CONTROL

Monitoring · Errors · Escalation

Review API and Reloadly issues, identify route/API patterns, prepare error-ratio evidence, communicate with partners and follow up on technical, pricing and service-level constraints.

03FINANCIAL OPERATIONS

Accounting · Reconciliation · Evidence

Support accounting portal postings, bank-balance checks and agent-balance reconciliation while building stronger repeatable controls, exception handling and evidence retention.

04REPORTING & MANAGEMENT

Profitability · Performance · Dashboards

Prepare management analysis, profit reporting, team-performance views, API margin records and operational dashboards that connect volume, sales, cost, profit, reliability and pricing.

05TEAM & CONTINUITY

Support · Training · Problem Solving

Handle agent and system issues, provide backup operational coverage, support queries and deposits, train colleagues on operations and comparison workflows, and keep work moving when issues cross team boundaries.

06CROSS-BUSINESS SUPPORT

RemitRio · Partner Analysis

Extend reporting and dashboard methods into RemitRio support, including fee-comparison work, corridor/agent reporting and operational-constraint analysis.
